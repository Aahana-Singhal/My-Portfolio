Beyond the Classroom

My hobbies allow me to explore different sides of myself. Whether I am reading a book, practising German, playing the piano, or training in Taekwondo, each activity brings a new learning experience.

Reading Books

Books take me into new worlds, introduce me to fascinating ideas, and inspire my imagination. Reading is one of my favourite ways to learn beyond the classroom.

Learning German

I use Duolingo to practise German, discover new vocabulary, and learn useful phrases. I enjoy the challenge of understanding and communicating in another language.

Playing the Piano

Music gives me a creative outlet and helps me develop concentration, patience, and a sense of rhythm. I enjoy learning and improving through regular practice.

Practising Taekwondo

Taekwondo helps me work on my focus, coordination, confidence, and discipline. It reminds me that improvement comes with dedication and perseverance.

My Perfect Mix

I love the balance between intellectual challenges, creative expression, language learning, and physical activity. Each hobby teaches me something different and makes my learning journey more exciting.
